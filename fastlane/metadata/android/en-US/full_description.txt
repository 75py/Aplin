<p><i>Aplin</i> is an application manager for your Android smartphone. It gives you details about your apps, such as first install, last update, version, permissions and package name. To make it easier for you to find an app, they can be grouped to show you either All, only System apps, only user apps, only apps you can Disable, only apps with Internet permissions, only apps that can "Draw over other apps".</p>
